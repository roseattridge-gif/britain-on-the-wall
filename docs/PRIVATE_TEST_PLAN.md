# BOTW V1 private test plan

## Gate

Run with 10–15 UK adults who have not worked on BOTW. Do not call the public launch approved until every participant session is logged, the scorecard is completed, no critical dimension is below 4/5, and no unresolved severity-one evidence or comprehension issue remains.

## Session

Use the production URL on the participant’s own phone or laptop. Do not explain the interface before the first task. Record device, viewport, task completion, wrong turns, quoted interpretation and confidence; do not collect sensitive personal data.

1. In 20 seconds, say what the main Wall is showing.
2. Find how much of every £100 goes to Health and social care.
3. Explain borrowing in your own words.
4. Use What Changed to describe one change without claiming a cause.
5. Check the source and limits for the tax-gap figure.
6. Ask “What happened to net migration?” and show the result on the Wall.
7. Ask an unsupported education question. What do you think the response means?
8. Complete the immigration handover journey. What was inherited?
9. Complete the health journey. Did higher spending improve outcomes?
10. Find one number you trust and explain why.

## Questions after tasks

- What is BOTW for?
- Which number, label or interaction was hardest to understand?
- Did any wording imply a party or policy caused a change?
- Could you distinguish UK fiscal data from England operational data?
- Did “estimate”, “provisional”, “not yet available” and “cannot prove” feel clear?
- Where would you go next: Britain, What Changed or Ask Britain?
- On a 1–5 scale: clarity, trust, evidence access, usefulness, neutrality, mobile readability.

## Issue rubric

- Severity 1: wrong number/source, broken route, inaccessible core task, causal/partisan misreading created by copy.
- Severity 2: task completes only after material confusion or evidence boundary is missed.
- Severity 3: polish/friction that does not change interpretation.

Fix and retest every severity-one issue. Retest a representative subset after severity-two changes.
