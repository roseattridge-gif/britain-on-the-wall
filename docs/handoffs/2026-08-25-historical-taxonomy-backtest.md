# Handoff — historical fiscal taxonomy backtest

The national taxonomy now has a normalized five-outturn historical backbone for both money out and money in. It is a data and interpretation increment only; no public timeline behaviour or Wall geometry changed.

Acceptance evidence:

- 14 expenditure destinations use unchanged formulas in every period.
- Each output total equals published TME exactly; unrounded shares equal £100.
- Five input pools plus derived borrowing also equal TME.
- Every flow carries source identifiers, official-line identifiers and comparability metadata.
- No unexplained expenditure residual or duplicate official-line mapping exists.
- £0.001bn PESA display-rounding differences are explicit in two years.
- 2022–23 energy/cost-of-living effects, debt composition and technical reconciliation have interpretation notes.
- Nominal history is frozen; real-terms conversion and public interaction are deliberately deferred.
