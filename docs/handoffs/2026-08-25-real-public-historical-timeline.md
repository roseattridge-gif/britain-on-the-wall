# Handoff — real public historical timeline

The frozen five-period national fiscal history is now exposed through the persistent Wall. Users can select or keyboard-scrub discrete outturns, play the sequence once, show computed earliest/latest changes, switch between Every £100 and current-price £bn, and inspect a five-year series without leaving spatial context.

No category moves position and no fiscal value is interpolated. Borrowing and technical reconciliation retain distinct semantics. Demo attention stories are absent from real mode; outcomes, leaks and deep Health remain static and explicitly illustrative.

Verification evidence and limitations are recorded in `docs/HISTORICAL_TIMELINE.md`.
