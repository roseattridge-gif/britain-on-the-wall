# Health data

## Domain boundary

The national territory remains **Health & social care**. On the PESA 2026 functional basis it is the sum of Health and Personal social services. Its drill-down uses four published sub-functions: Medical services, Personal social services, Central and other health services, and Medical research. It is not labelled “the NHS”.

## Geography

The reconciled allocation is UK public-sector spending. The deeper activity and access measures are an explicitly separate **England operational lens**, because health delivery is devolved and no consistent UK operational series was found that matches the PESA boundary. England metrics do not reconcile to the UK total.

## Fiscal allocation and spending composition

All values are nominal current-price £bn from PESA 2026 Table 5.2.

The displayed lines reconcile to the Health & social care parent to within £0.001bn, the published-table rounding tolerance. No operational NHS dataset is forced into that reconciliation.

## Outputs

- GP appointments booked: 369.7m in the 12 months to March 2024 and 383.4m to March 2025.
- A&E attendances: 26.321m in 2023–24 and 27.368m in 2024–25, derived from the published average monthly totals multiplied by 12.
- Community diagnostic centre tests: 6.8m in 2024–25. No prior value is asserted.

These are volumes of activity, not measures of health improvement.

## Outcomes and context

- Elective waiting list: 7.54m pathways in March 2024; 7.42m in March 2025. Lower is better.
- Incomplete pathways waiting no longer than 18 weeks: 57.2% to 59.8%. Higher is better.
- Diagnostic waits of six weeks or more: 21.8% to 18.4%. Lower is better.
- Annual A&E attendances over 12 hours: 9.7% to 10.4%. Lower is better.
- Positive GP practice experience: 73.9% in the 2024 survey; 75.4% in 2025. Higher is better and classified as context only.

Status compares the selected value with the latest earlier stored value and applies the declared polarity. Neutral activity metrics receive no improving/deteriorating judgement. There is no composite Health score.

## Historical periods

Fiscal values use financial years from 2021–22 to 2025–26. Operational measures retain their actual financial-year, rolling-12-month, month-end or survey period. Missing observations remain unavailable; they are not interpolated.

## Spend, output and outcome relationship

The Wall places the UK fiscal allocation beside England activity and outcome measures to support inspection. This is not a causal model. It does not claim that a change in expenditure caused a change in waiting times, experience or activity.

## Return classification

Published activity volumes are **Measured return**. Access and waiting measures are **Indicative return** because they can sensibly be viewed with system activity but attribution is uncertain. GP experience is **Context only**. No broad outcome is called ROI.

## Evidence sources

- HM Treasury, PESA 2026 Chapter 5 Table 5.2, accredited official statistics.
- Department of Health and Social Care, Annual Report and Accounts 2024 to 2025, Performance Report; its measures cite the underlying NHS England collections.

Each rendered metric uses the existing evidence drawer and records source, definition, period, geography, revision status, limitations, status and confidence.

## Known limitations

- UK allocation and England delivery have different boundaries and must not be divided to produce unit costs.
- Patient pathways are not unique people.
- A&E attendance totals are derived from rounded monthly averages.
- GP survey results before 2024 are not directly comparable because methodology changed.
- Operational values currently cover only the strongest two-year comparison exposed by the annual report, not the full fiscal timeline.

## Unresolved items

No unit economics are shown because matching official numerators and denominators were not established. Hospitals remains the visual entry to the England operational lens, not a quantified UK hospital allocation. Illustrative leak/waste figures remain explicitly illustrative and outside this real Health model.
