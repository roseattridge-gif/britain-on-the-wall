# Health system data contract

## Accepted snapshots

All operational points are England NHS official statistics aligned to the March end of each Wall fiscal period.

| Fiscal period | RTT waiting list | Within 18 weeks | NHS HCHS workforce FTE | Completed RTT pathways in March |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2021–22 | 6,365,772 | 62.2% | 1,226,677 | 1,439,589 |
| 2022–23 | 7,331,186 | 58.6% | 1,280,350 | 1,536,762 |
| 2023–24 | 7,538,830 | 57.2% | 1,345,047 | 1,429,572 |
| 2024–25 | 7,418,598 | 59.8% | 1,378,470 | 1,535,984 |
| 2025–26 | 7,106,091 | 65.3% | 1,379,670 | 1,711,408 |

RTT values use NHS England's national series including estimates for missing trusts where supplied. Workforce is full-time equivalent Hospital and Community Health Service staff in NHS trusts and other core organisations; primary care is excluded.

## Sources and revisions

- NHS England RTT statistics user guidance and the published RTT overview time series.
- NHS Workforce Statistics, March releases for 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026.
- HM Treasury PESA 2026 Table 5.2 through the existing national fiscal data layer.

Every operational point stores source IDs, actual date, unit, official status and comparability. The 2023–24 point is MEDIUM because of the documented March 2024 RTT scope change; other snapshots are HIGH within the limits above.
